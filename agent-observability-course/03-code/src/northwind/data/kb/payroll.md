# Payroll dates, payslips and tax documents

- Salaries are paid on the **25th** of each month (the previous business day when the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday).
- Payslips appear in Workday two days before payday. Historical payslips are kept for 10 years.
- Changes to bank details must be submitted in Workday by the **10th** to take effect that month.
- Overtime and shift premiums approved by the 5th are paid in the same month; later approvals roll into the next run.
- Annual tax statements are published in Workday by **31 January**.
- Annual bonus (eligible roles) is paid with the **March** payroll after year-end results are approved.

## Questions about a payslip
Open a ticket with category *HR / Payroll*. Include the pay period; do not include your bank details in the ticket.

## Payroll calendar
| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| 5th | Cut-off for overtime, shift premiums and expense claims to be included in this month's pay |
| 10th | Cut-off for bank detail changes, tax form changes and new benefit elections |
| 23rd | Payslips published in Workday |
| 25th | Salary paid (previous business day if the 25th is a weekend or public holiday) |
| 31 January | Annual tax statements published |
| March payroll | Annual bonus for eligible roles |

## Reading your payslip
The payslip shows base salary, shift premiums, overtime, allowances (connectivity, commuting), taxable
benefits (bike lease, company car), employee pension contribution, tax, social security and the net amount.
Corrections from previous months appear as separate lines labelled *retro*. A glossary is linked at the
bottom of the payslip in Workday.

## Overtime and shift premiums
Overtime must be approved in advance by the shift supervisor in the time system; approved hours are paid at
125% on weekdays, 150% on Saturdays and 200% on Sundays and public holidays. Night shift premium is 20%
between 22:00 and 06:00. Office staff on salaried contracts do not receive overtime pay; they take time in
lieu by agreement with their manager.

## Changing bank details
Bank details are changed only by the employee in Workday (*Pay → Payment Elections*) with MFA. Payroll will
never change bank details based on an e-mail request; this is a common fraud pattern. Changes made after
the 10th take effect the following month.

## Frequently asked questions
- **My net pay is lower this month.** Check the retro lines and the tax code first; a *HR / Payroll* ticket
  with the pay period gets an explanation within one business day.
- **Can I get a salary advance?** In hardship cases HR can approve an advance of up to one month's net pay,
  repaid over three months. Ask your HR business partner.
- **Where do I find payslips from before Workday?** Archived payslips (older than 2022) are requested with a
  *HR / Payroll* ticket and provided as PDF within five business days.

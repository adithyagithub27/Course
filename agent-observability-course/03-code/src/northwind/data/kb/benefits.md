# Benefits: pension, health insurance and commuting

- **Pension**: Northwind contributes 6% of base salary; employees contribute a minimum of 3% and may increase in Workday during the November enrolment window.
- **Health insurance**: group plan with the national provider; dependants can be added within 30 days of joining or of a life event.
- **Commuting**: public transport is reimbursed at 100% up to €120 per month; the bike-lease scheme allows up to €3,000 gross salary sacrifice over 36 months.
- **Employee assistance programme**: free confidential counselling, 24/7, phone 0800-555-0199.
- **Learning budget**: €1,000 per year for courses and certifications, approved by your manager in Workday.

## Pension
The pension plan is a defined contribution scheme with the national provider. Northwind contributes 6% of
base salary; the employee contributes at least 3% and may raise this to 10% during the November enrolment
window or within 30 days of joining. Contributions are deducted from gross salary. Statements are available
in the provider portal linked from Workday. Transfers of previous pensions into the plan are possible once a
year; the provider's helpline handles the paperwork.

## Health insurance
The group plan covers employees on permanent and fixed-term contracts from day one. Dependants (partner,
children under 18 or in full-time education) can be added within 30 days of joining or of a life event such
as birth or marriage, otherwise during the November window. The plan includes dental basic cover and
physiotherapy; premiums for dependants are deducted from net salary. Claims are submitted in the insurer's
app; questions go to the insurer first and to HR Total Rewards only for eligibility issues.

## Commuting and mobility
Public transport is reimbursed at 100% up to EUR 120 per month against a subscription or receipts uploaded in
Workday. Car commuting is reimbursed at EUR 0.19 per kilometre for distances over 10 km, up to 75 km per
day, on office days recorded in the hybrid agreement. The bike-lease scheme allows up to EUR 3,000 gross
salary sacrifice over 36 months for a bike or e-bike, including insurance and maintenance; the bike may be
bought at residual value at the end. Parking at the Rotterdam office is limited and allocated to shift staff
and employees with mobility needs first.

## Wellbeing and learning
The employee assistance programme offers free, confidential counselling by phone (0800-555-0199) and online,
24/7, for employees and household members, up to 8 sessions per issue. The learning budget of EUR 1,000 per
year covers courses, certifications, conferences and books related to the current or a plausible next role;
requests are approved by the manager in Workday and paid through Concur or by invoice. Unused budget does
not carry over. Language courses for Dutch, German and English are additionally free of charge.

## Frequently asked questions
- **When can I change my benefit elections?** During the November window, or within 30 days of a life event.
- **Is the bike lease taxable?** A small taxable benefit applies; the calculator in Workday shows the net cost.
- **Does the learning budget cover a university degree?** Partially; degrees follow the study agreement
  policy with a repayment clause if you leave within two years.

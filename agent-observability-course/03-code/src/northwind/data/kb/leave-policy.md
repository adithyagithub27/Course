# Annual leave, sick leave and public holidays

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive **28 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, pro-rated for
part-time and for joiners. Up to **5 unused days** carry over to the next year and must be
used by 31 March. Request leave in Workday at least two weeks ahead for periods over three days;
your manager approves within five working days.

## Sick leave
Notify your manager before your usual start time on day one. From the **4th consecutive day**
a doctor's note is required and must be uploaded to Workday. Sick leave does not reduce your
annual leave balance.

## Public holidays
Public holidays follow the country of your employment contract. Warehouse shift staff working a
public holiday receive a day in lieu plus the shift premium defined in the collective agreement.

## Special leave
Marriage (2 days), bereavement of close family (up to 5 days), moving house (1 day) and
volunteering (2 days per year) are paid special leave and are requested through the same Workday flow.

## Requesting and approving leave
Requests are made in Workday under *Absence → Request Absence*. Periods of up to three days can be
requested at any time; longer periods must be requested at least two weeks ahead. Managers approve or
decline within five working days; if they do not act, the request escalates to their manager. Teams may
agree blackout periods (for example peak season in the warehouses, November to mid-January) during which
only pre-approved leave is possible. Leave is booked in half-day units.

## Part-time, joiners and leavers
Entitlement is pro-rated by contract hours and by the months worked in the year. A joiner starting on 1 July
with a full-time contract receives 14 days for that year. Leavers are paid out for unused leave with the
final salary; negative balances are deducted. Unpaid leave of up to 3 months per 5 years of service may be
approved by the department head for study, family or travel reasons.

## Sick leave details
Notify your manager before your start time on each day of absence for the first three days; from the
fourth consecutive day upload a doctor's note in Workday. Long-term absence (over 4 weeks) is managed with
HR and the occupational health provider, including a return-to-work plan. Sick days during booked annual
leave are re-credited if a doctor's note covers them. Caring for a sick child or partner is covered by
*short-term care leave* (up to 10 days per year at 70% pay) and is requested through the same flow.

## Public holidays and shift work
Public holidays follow the country of the employment contract. Office staff have the day off. Warehouse and
transport staff who work a public holiday receive a day in lieu plus the shift premium in the collective
labour agreement. Regional holidays (for example Carnival in Limburg) are not company holidays.

## Frequently asked questions
- **Can I sell or buy leave days?** Up to 5 days can be bought through salary sacrifice during the
  November enrolment window; days cannot be sold.
- **What happens to carried-over days I do not use by 31 March?** They lapse.
- **Do I accrue leave while on parental leave?** Yes, statutory accrual continues.

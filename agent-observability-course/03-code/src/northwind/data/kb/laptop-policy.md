# Laptop and hardware policy

- Standard issue is a 14" business laptop; engineers and designers may request the 16" performance model with manager approval.
- Laptops are refreshed every **4 years** or when repair cost exceeds 50% of replacement value.
- Accessories (monitor, dock, headset, keyboard, mouse) up to **€400 per year** can be ordered in ServiceHub without approval.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported within **24 hours** to the service desk so the device can be remotely wiped. File a police report for theft; HR needs the reference number.
- Personal devices may access e-mail and chat through the managed browser only; no VPN from personal devices.
- Damage caused by negligence (liquid spills, drops without a case) may be charged back to the department at the manager's discretion.

## Requesting a replacement
Open a ticket with category *Hardware / Replacement*, include the asset tag (sticker on the underside, format `NWL-######`) and the fault description. Loaners are available at the Rotterdam and Hamburg IT desks.

## Standard models
The standard laptop is a 14-inch business model with 16 GB RAM and 512 GB storage. Engineers, data staff,
designers and video editors may request the 16-inch performance model (32 GB RAM, dedicated graphics)
with manager approval; the request is a *Hardware / New device* ticket with the business reason. Warehouse
and transport roles that share workstations do not receive personal laptops but may be issued a rugged
tablet by their site lead. Two monitors, a docking station and a headset are standard for office desks.

## Ordering accessories
Accessories up to EUR 400 per calendar year are ordered from the ServiceHub catalogue without approval:
monitors (up to 27 inches), docks, keyboards, mice, headsets, laptop stands, webcams and cables. Items
outside the catalogue or above the yearly allowance need manager approval and are ordered by IT Asset
Management. Home-office furniture is covered by the hybrid work policy, not by this allowance.

## Repairs, loaners and replacements
1. Report the fault in a *Hardware / Replacement* ticket with the asset tag (NWL-######) and a description.
2. The service desk triages within one business day. Simple repairs (keyboard, battery, charger) are done
   on site in Rotterdam and Hamburg; other sites ship the device by courier with a prepaid label.
3. Loaner laptops are available at the Rotterdam and Hamburg IT desks and can be couriered to other sites
   within two business days. Loaners must be returned within 5 days of receiving the repaired device.
4. Devices older than 4 years, or whose repair costs more than half of the replacement value, are replaced
   rather than repaired.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report within 24 hours through Atlas, ServiceHub or extension 4000 so the device can be remotely locked
and wiped. For theft, file a police report; HR needs the reference number for the insurance claim. A
replacement is issued as a loaner first and as a permanent device once the claim is processed. Employees
are not charged for loss or theft unless gross negligence is established.

## Frequently asked questions
- **Can I buy the laptop when I leave?** No. All devices are returned on the last working day; the
  leaver checklist in Workday lists the return address.
- **Can I use my own monitor at home and expense it?** Yes, within the EUR 400 accessory allowance or the
  EUR 300 home-office equipment allowance, not both for the same item.
- **My child spilled juice on the laptop. Am I liable?** Accidental damage is covered; repeated
  negligence may be charged back to the department at the manager's discretion.
- **How do I find the asset tag?** The white sticker on the underside, or *Company Portal → Devices*.

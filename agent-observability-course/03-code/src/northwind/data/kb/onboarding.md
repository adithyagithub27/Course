# New joiner onboarding checklist

## Before day one (manager)
- Raise the *New Joiner* request in ServiceHub at least 10 working days before the start date.
- Choose laptop model, phone (if needed), and the application bundle for the role.
- Assign a buddy and book the first-week meetings.

## Day one (employee)
1. Collect your laptop and badge at the IT desk (Rotterdam HQ, floor 2) or receive the courier package.
2. Set your password at `okta.northwind.example/activate` and enrol Okta Verify.
3. Complete the *Welcome to Northwind* e-learning (45 minutes) and the security awareness module.
4. Check your Workday profile: address, bank details, emergency contact, tax form.

## First month
- Probation goals agreed in Workday by day 14.
- Mandatory training: Code of Conduct, Data Protection, Safety in the Warehouse (operations roles).
- Payroll: you are paid on the 25th; if you started after the 15th the first payment is in the following month.

## Timeline for managers
| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Offer accepted | HR creates the Workday record; the manager receives the *New Joiner* task |
| At least 10 working days before start | Manager submits the *New Joiner* request in ServiceHub: laptop model, phone, application bundle, seat, badge access zones |
| 5 working days before | IT prepares the device; HR sends the welcome pack and the activation link |
| Day 1 | Badge and laptop handover, Okta activation, Welcome session |
| Week 1 | Buddy meetings, team introductions, mandatory security training |
| Day 14 | Probation goals agreed in Workday |
| Month 1 | Mandatory e-learning complete; first check-in with HR |
| Month 3 and 6 | Probation reviews |

## Application bundles
The *Office* bundle (Microsoft 365, Slack, Zoom, ServiceHub, Workday, Concur) is assigned to everyone.
Role bundles add systems: *Operations* (TMS, Safety app, warehouse scanners), *Finance* (ERP, Concur
approver, banking portal), *Engineering* (GitHub, cloud console, JetBrains), *HR* (Workday admin,
recruiting tool). Extra access is requested by the manager with an *Access* ticket after the start date.

## Remote joiners
Employees who start remotely receive the laptop by courier two days before the start date with a
printed quick-start guide. The activation call with the service desk is booked for 09:00 on day one.
Badges are issued at the first office visit.

## Frequently asked questions
- **When is the first salary paid?** On the 25th of the starting month if you started on or before the
  15th; otherwise with the following month's payroll, including the partial month.
- **Which trainings are mandatory in the first month?** Code of Conduct, Data Protection and Security
  Awareness for everyone; Safety in the Warehouse for operations roles; Anti-bribery for finance and
  procurement roles.
- **Can I bring my own keyboard or mouse?** Yes, personal peripherals without storage are allowed.
- **Who is my buddy?** Your manager assigns one before day one; the name is in the welcome e-mail.

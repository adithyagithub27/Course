# Hybrid and remote work policy

Office-based roles work a **hybrid** pattern: at least **2 days per week** in the office, agreed
with the team. Warehouse and transport roles are on site by nature.

## Working from abroad
Up to **20 working days per year** from another EU country with manager approval recorded in
Workday. Longer periods or non-EU countries need HR and tax approval and are rarely granted.

## Home office allowance
A one-off **€300** equipment allowance (see *Expense claims*) and a monthly **€30** connectivity
allowance paid through payroll for employees with a signed hybrid agreement.

## Ergonomics
Request a home workstation assessment through ServiceHub. Northwind provides a second monitor
and a chair on request for employees working from home two or more days per week.

## The hybrid agreement
Employees in office-based roles sign a hybrid agreement in Workday that records the agreed office days and
the home workplace address (needed for insurance and tax). The agreement is reviewed once a year and can be
changed with one month's notice by either side. Teams agree anchor days when everyone is in the office;
managers may require additional office days for onboarding, workshops or performance reasons.

## Equipment and allowances
A one-off EUR 300 equipment allowance can be spent on a desk, chair, monitor or lighting and is claimed
through Concur with receipts. Northwind also provides, on request, a second monitor and an ergonomic chair
from the standard catalogue for employees working two or more days from home. The monthly EUR 30
connectivity allowance is paid through payroll for the duration of the hybrid agreement.

## Working from abroad
Up to 20 working days per calendar year from another EU or EEA country are allowed with manager approval
recorded in Workday (*Request Remote Work Abroad*). Requests must be submitted at least two weeks before
travel and include the country and dates. Longer periods, non-EU countries and any arrangement that would
make another country the main place of work require HR and tax approval and are rarely granted because of
payroll tax and social security consequences. Work from sanctioned countries is prohibited.

## Health, safety and security at home
The employee is responsible for a safe workplace at home: a stable desk and chair, adequate light, and a
private space for calls involving Confidential data. Company equipment must not be used by family members.
Use the VPN for intranet systems and never print Confidential documents at home.

## Frequently asked questions
- **Can I work fully remote?** Only for roles advertised as remote or by exception approved by the department
  head and HR.
- **Do office days have to be the same each week?** Not necessarily; the agreement records the number of
  days, and the team plans the pattern.
- **Is my commute time working time on office days?** No.

# Expense claims and reimbursement

Submit expenses in **Concur** within **30 days** of the spend. Claims older than 90 days are rejected.

## What you need
- Itemised receipts for anything above **€25** (photos are fine).
- A cost centre and a business purpose in the description.
- For client entertainment: attendee names and companies.

## Limits
| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Hotel (EU) | €160 per night |
| Hotel (US) | $220 per night |
| Meals while travelling | €60 per day or the country per-diem |
| Mileage (own car) | €0.30 per km |
| Home office equipment | €300 one-off, approval required |

## Payment
Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run or, for amounts over €500, within 10
working days by bank transfer. Corporate card transactions must still be matched to a
report in Concur or they are deducted from salary after 60 days.

## Submitting a claim step by step
1. Open Concur (web or mobile app) and start a new report named `<month> <purpose>`.
2. Add each expense: photograph the receipt, choose the category and the cost centre, and enter the business
   purpose. Corporate card transactions appear automatically after two days and only need a receipt and a
   purpose.
3. For client meals add the attendee names and companies; for travel add the trip dates and destination.
4. Submit. Your manager approves in Concur; Finance audits a sample and may ask for clarification.

## Travel booking and policy
Flights, trains and hotels are booked through the travel tool linked in Concur; bookings made elsewhere
are reimbursed only up to the policy price. Economy class applies to flights under 6 hours; premium economy
may be booked for longer flights. Trains are preferred for trips under 5 hours within Europe. Rental cars
need a business reason and the compact category unless equipment is carried. Travel insurance is provided
automatically for booked trips.

## Per diems
Instead of individual meal receipts, employees on multi-day trips may claim the country per diem: EUR 60 in
the Netherlands, EUR 55 in Germany, EUR 65 in France and Italy, EUR 45 in Poland, USD 75 in the United
States. Per diems are reduced by 30% for each meal provided by the hotel, a client or a conference.

## Not reimbursable
Alcohol outside client entertainment, minibar items, parking fines, personal entertainment, laundry on trips
under 5 nights, upgrades, and expenses for family members. Tips above 10% are not reimbursed.

## Frequently asked questions
- **I lost a receipt above EUR 25.** Submit a *missing receipt declaration* in Concur; up to three per year
  are accepted without further questions.
- **My claim was rejected without a reason.** The reason is in the report comments in Concur; open an
  *HR / Payroll* or *Finance* ticket if you cannot see it.
- **When will I be paid?** Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run (25th). Claims over EUR 500
  approved by the 15th are paid within 10 working days by bank transfer.
- **Can I claim my home internet?** No; the EUR 30 monthly connectivity allowance in the hybrid policy covers it.

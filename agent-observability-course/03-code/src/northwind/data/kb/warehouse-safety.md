# Warehouse safety essentials

- Safety shoes and a high-visibility vest are mandatory in all warehouse zones; hearing protection in the sortation hall.
- Forklift operation requires a valid internal licence renewed every 3 years; visitors must stay in the marked walkways.
- Report every **near miss** in the Safety app within the shift; the shift supervisor reviews it within 24 hours.
- Injuries: first aiders are listed at every fire point; call extension 2222 for the on-site medic; the supervisor files the accident report the same day.
- Manual lifting limit is 23 kg per person; use the lifting aid or ask a colleague.
- Temperatures below 5 °C in the cold store: 15-minute warm-up break every 90 minutes.

## Personal protective equipment by zone
| Zone | Required PPE |
|---|---|
| All warehouse areas | Safety shoes (S3), high-visibility vest |
| Sortation hall | Plus hearing protection |
| Loading docks | Plus gloves; hard hat when cranes or racking work is in progress |
| Cold store (below 5 C) | Plus insulated jacket, gloves and hat; 15-minute warm-up break every 90 minutes |
| Battery charging area | Plus safety glasses; no metal jewellery |
PPE is issued by the site lead on day one and replaced on request; damaged PPE must not be used.

## Vehicles and equipment
Forklifts, reach trucks and pallet movers may only be operated with a valid internal licence, renewed every
three years with a practical test. Pedestrians stay inside the marked walkways; vehicles have priority in
marked aisles. Speed is limited to 10 km/h indoors and 20 km/h in the yard. Pre-shift checks are recorded
in the Safety app; a vehicle with a failed check is tagged out. Phones are not used while operating
equipment.

## Manual handling
The lifting limit is 23 kg per person; heavier items are lifted with equipment or by two people. Use the
lifting aids at the packing stations, keep loads close to the body and avoid twisting. Report pain or
discomfort early to the shift supervisor; ergonomics assessments are available for every workstation.

## Incidents, injuries and near misses
- **Near miss** (nothing happened, but it could have): report in the Safety app before the end of the shift.
  The supervisor reviews within 24 hours; near-miss reports are never used for discipline.
- **First aid**: first aiders are listed at every fire point; call extension 2222 for the on-site medic.
- **Injury**: the supervisor files the accident report the same day; the employee sees the occupational
  health provider within 48 hours for anything beyond first aid. Serious accidents are reported to the
  authorities by Health & Safety within 24 hours.
- **Fire or evacuation**: follow the wardens to the assembly point; do not use lifts; do not return for
  belongings.

## Frequently asked questions
- **Can visitors enter the warehouse?** Only escorted, with a vest and safety shoes; visitor shoes are
  available at reception.
- **I lost my forklift licence card.** The Safety app shows your licence status; a replacement card is
  issued by the site lead.
- **Who pays for prescription safety glasses?** Northwind, up to EUR 150 every two years, through the PPE
  request in ServiceHub.

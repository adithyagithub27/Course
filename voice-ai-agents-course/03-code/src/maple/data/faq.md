# Maple Street Dental FAQ

Fictional clinic used in the course "Production Voice AI Agents with Python". Each `##` section is
one retrievable answer. Keep answers short and speakable: Riley reads them aloud.

## Opening hours
We are open Monday to Thursday from eight in the morning to five in the evening, with a lunch break from noon to one. On Fridays we are open from eight to two. On Saturdays we are open from nine to one. We are closed on Sundays and on major public holidays.

## Address and directions
Maple Street Dental is at 1420 Maple Street, Suite 200, Austin, Texas, on the second floor of the Maple Commons building, across the street from Riverside Park. There is an elevator next to the main entrance.

## Parking
Free parking is available in the Maple Commons garage behind the building. Bring your parking ticket to the front desk and we will validate it for up to two hours. Street parking on Maple Street is metered. There are two accessible parking spaces next to the garage elevator.

## Insurance accepted
We are in network with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, Guardian and United Concordia PPO plans. We also accept most other PPO plans as out of network and will file the claim for you. We do not accept HMO or Medicaid dental plans. Please bring your insurance card to every visit.

## New patient forms
New patients should arrive fifteen minutes early. You can fill in the new patient forms online through the link in your confirmation text, or on paper at the front desk. Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of any medications you take.

## Cancellation policy
Please give us at least twenty four hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged a fifty dollar fee. You can cancel or reschedule by phone at any time with Riley, our AI assistant.

## Dental emergencies
If you have trouble breathing, severe swelling of your face or throat, uncontrolled bleeding, or a head injury, hang up and call nine one one right away. For urgent dental problems like a knocked out tooth, a broken tooth, or severe tooth pain, call us and we will offer the earliest same day or next day appointment. After hours, our emergency line is five one two, five five five, zero one nine nine.

## Services offered
We offer checkups and cleanings, fillings, crowns and bridges, root canals, extractions, teeth whitening, clear aligners, night guards, and children's dentistry. We refer patients to specialists for oral surgery like wisdom tooth removal under general anesthesia, and for dental implants.

## Pricing ranges
Prices depend on your insurance and on what the dentist finds. Without insurance, a new patient exam with x-rays and a cleaning is usually between one hundred fifty and two hundred fifty dollars. A tooth colored filling is usually between one hundred fifty and three hundred dollars. A crown usually costs between one thousand and one thousand five hundred dollars. Riley can only give ranges; the front desk can give you a written estimate.

## Payment options and payment plans
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, HSA and FSA cards, and CareCredit. For treatment over five hundred dollars we offer interest free payment plans over six or twelve months. Payment is due at the time of your visit for the portion insurance does not cover.

## Children and families
We see children from their first birthday. Parents or guardians must come with patients under eighteen for their first visit and sign the consent form. Family members can book back to back appointments so everyone is seen on the same day.

## Accessibility
Our office is wheelchair accessible, with an elevator, wide doorways and an accessible restroom. Service animals are welcome. If you need extra time, a sign language interpreter, or any other accommodation, tell us when you book and we will arrange it.

## Languages spoken
Our team speaks English and Spanish. Doctor Alvarez and two of our hygienists are fluent in Spanish. For other languages we can arrange a phone interpreter at no cost if you tell us when you book.

## Illness and COVID policy
If you have a fever, cough, or flu like symptoms, please reschedule your visit; there is no late cancellation fee when you are sick. Masks are available at the front desk. We clean and disinfect every treatment room between patients.

## Appointment reminders
We send a reminder text two days before your appointment and a reminder call the day before. Reply C to the text to confirm. You can ask the front desk to change how you get reminders or to stop reminder calls.

## Contact and front desk
Our main phone number is five one two, five five five, zero one zero zero. The front desk team is available during opening hours. You can also email hello at maple street dental dot example. Riley, our AI assistant, answers calls around the clock and can transfer you to a person during opening hours.

## Our dentists
Doctor Maya Chen is our lead dentist and has practiced for fifteen years. Doctor Luis Alvarez specializes in root canals and restorative work. Doctor Hannah Brooks focuses on children's dentistry and clear aligners.

## Ranking Criteria

Each option is evaluated on:

- **Keyword match** — Does it contain high-volume search terms students actually type?
- **Clarity** — Can a student understand exactly what they'll learn in under 5 seconds?
- **Outcome promise** — Does it promise a tangible result (not just knowledge)?
- **Differentiation** — Does it stand out from competing courses?
- **Udemy SEO** — Will the Udemy algorithm surface it for relevant searches?

## (a) Executive summary: the 10 issues that matter most, ranked by learner impact

1. **The running example changes numbers halfway through the course.** Sections 1–5 narrate a replayed day of **$6.42, 1,184 conversations, 412 personas, seed 20260928, p95 4.1 s** (S2:L376–380, L401–407; S3:L750; S4:L352; S5:L331). Section 6 onward narrates a different day: **$56.70, 10,184 requests, 4,000 sessions, seed 7** (S6:L14–29; S7:L18–24; S8:L18; S9:L17). The code produces the second day (`make replay` → **$56.28, p95 3,827 ms**; Makefile `SEED ?= 7`, `SESSIONS ?= 4000`). Lab 1 already uses $56.70/3827 ms (lab-01:L219), and quiz 2 Q4 invents a third day ("about 2,200 requests and $35", "`--seed 42` by default", quizzes/section-02.md:L63,L68). A learner who writes "$6.42, 41% ops" in the build log as told in 2.4 cannot reconcile it with Section 6, 2.4's "your answer should be $6.42" claim (S2:L385) is false, and the Section 6 figures are themselves ~1% off what the mock produces ($56.70 vs $56.28; caching $37.24 vs $37.00; all three $19.21 vs $19.07).

2. **Sections 4 and 5 teach an instrumentation path the shipped Atlas does not use.** The code-alongs type `@observe(as_type=...)`, `get_client().update_current_generation(...)`, `propagate_attributes(...)` in the `/chat` route, `score_current_trace`, `start_as_current_observation(as_type="chain")`, `create_event`, `score_current_span` (S4:L173–196, L300–313, L547–557, L816–836; S5:L143–158, L185–196, L400–439). None of these appear in `app/`: `grep` finds them only as wrappers in `telemetry/langfuse_setup.py` and in `simulator/replay.py:L534–544`. The real agent records generations with `self.tracer.start_as_current_span(...)` plus `ga.set_llm_usage/set_cost` (`app/agent.py:L887–920`), which is what Sections 6, 7 and 14 then show (S6:L512–565; S14:L262–300). A student who follows 4.2–5.4 ends up with an agent that disagrees with every later lecture and with the test suite; CLAUDE.md says the code is right, so Sections 4–5 need rewriting around `trace_attributes()`, `ga.*` and the shipped `_loop`.

3. **The headline "guards off" demo cannot be recorded as written.** 1.1 and 5.6 switch the step limit off with `ATLAS_MAX_STEPS=0` "(unlimited)" (S1:L46,L58; S5:L600,L607). In code, `for step in range(1, s.max_steps + 1)` (`app/agent.py:L783`) makes 0 mean *zero steps*: Atlas makes no model call at all. The same lectures say the default limit is 8 (S1:L101,L108; S5:L600,L229–231); the shipped default is 6 (`config.py:L98`, `.env.example`). The 61-step, $3.90 "Friday run" that the whole course keeps referring back to (S1, S5.6, S15.1) has no reproduction path.

4. **`make test` is not green and the failing test is the course's own anti-pattern.** `tests/integration/test_spans.py::test_chat_produces_agent_generation_tool_and_guardrail_spans` fails deterministically, in isolation and in the full run (328 passed, 1 failed), at L41: `root.parent is None` is false — the `invoke_agent atlas` span has a parent. Lecture 2.2 promises "168 passed" (S2:L173,L179; actual suite is 329 tests), CLAUDE.md says 329 green, and CI (`ci.yml` `test` job) would be red on day one. This is exactly the "orphan/parent" lie 3.6 teaches students to catch.

5. **Incident 1's replay-the-fix step and the Makefile's own example crash.** `make replay SCENARIO=cost_spike` raises `ValueError: ATLAS_SCENARIO='cost_spike' is not one of loop, ticket_flaky, …` because the Makefile exports `ATLAS_SCENARIO=$(SCENARIO)` (Makefile:L28) and `Settings.from_env` validates it (`config.py:L151`). The Makefile header advertises `make replay SCENARIO=cost_spike` (Makefile:L9), 11.2 ends with "Replay the `cost_spike` preset against the fix" (S11:L338), and 14.3 drives `make run … SCENARIO=context_bloat` (S14:L585). Only the five base scenario names work through `make`.

6. **Scripts cite 30+ symbols, tests, flags and files that do not exist in `03-code`.** Examples: tests `test_ticket_question_emits_tagged_tool_span`, `test_no_orphan_spans`, `test_one_generation_per_model_call`, `test_guardrail_observation`, `test_loop_scenario_stops_early`, `test_no_raw_pii_reaches_any_span` (S3:L543,L769–776; S4:L868; S5:L687; S10:L294) vs the real names (`test_spans.py:L18–215`); config fields `llm_first_token_timeout_s`, `llm_total_timeout_s`, `llm_max_retries` (S7:L835–837, then the same lecture uses the real `ATLAS_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_S` at L843); metrics `atlas_inflight`, `atlas_queue_wait_seconds`, `atlas_step_limit_total`, `atlas_budget_hits_total` (S7:L715; S5:L556–563); `MAX_INPUT_TOKENS_PER_GENERATION` and a "tokens test" in the budget gate (S13:L337,L387; the gate has four tests, none on tokens: `test_budget_gate.py:L42–83`); `python -m app.prompts register` (S4:L430; no `__main__`); `python -m evals.drift_report --prev 2026-W38 --curr 2026-W39` (S8:L690; args are `--baseline-store/--seed/--sessions`, `drift_report.py:L104–117`); `make replay PROM=1` (S9:L535; `PROM` only acts on `make run`, Makefile:L41–43); `northwind.guardrails.looks_like_injection` (S4:L817; lives in `app/mock_llm.py:L227`); `SCENARIO=ticket_escalation` (S5:L731; not in `INCIDENT_PRESETS`); `JUDGE_MAX_CALLS` (plan:L191, recording-guide:L161, qa:L114; not in `evals/online_judge.py`); `lf.update_prompt(name=, version=, new_labels=)` for the Incident 3 rollback (S11:L702) is not in the curriculum §6 verified list nor in `langfuse_setup.py`.

7. **Quality and SLO numbers contradict each other across sections and the code.** Judge scores: S6 says resolved 0.89 / grounded 0.94 (S6:L28); S8 says 0.83 / 0.90 (S8:L22); S9 says task success 83%, containment 78% (S9:L17–18); the replay reports judge_overall 0.912, grounded 0.943, task_success 0.993, containment 0.989 (`make console-text`). Latency: 5.4 says TTFT p50 ≈350 ms, p95 ≈1.1 s with a 312 ms demo (S5:L454–461); 7.1 says TTFT p50 0.55 s (S7:L18); the store says TTFT p50 1,427 ms, p95 1,702 ms and total p50 3,232 ms (not 1.9 s "first visible token" as in S7:L20). 9.1 says Atlas tightens fast-burn to 6×/30 m and that the textbook 14.4×/1 h "would not fire on a 45-minute event" (S9:L109–118); `deploy/alerts.yml:L13–35` and 9.5 (S9:L694–715) ship the textbook 14.4×/1 h and 6×/6 h rules, on task-success not latency. 14.3's narration ("latency burn rate above fourteen point four… tool error spike above twenty percent", S14:L581) contradicts the rule block it is reading (p95 > 4 s, cost 2.5×, tool error > 5 %, S14:L552–578).

8. **The Ops Console the scripts narrate does not exist.** 1.1 needs a "Live cost" page with a climbing meter, a steps counter, a context-size sparkline and an "Alerts" panel (S1:L50–101); 2.4 needs a Traces page with a waterfall and "ten most expensive conversations each linking to its trace" (S2:L397–415); 11.2 needs a traffic page "with last Monday as a shadow line" and a "retrieval page" (S11:L205–216); 8.3 a clickable disagreement table (S8:L482). Lab 6 points at `deploy/prometheus/alerts.yml` "with one example rule" and `deploy/prometheus/prometheus.yml` (lab-06:L9,L82,L149,L278); the real files are `deploy/alerts.yml` (already containing the `AtlasToolErrorRate` rule the lab asks the student to write) and `deploy/prometheus.yml` scraping `atlas:8000`, not `host.docker.internal`. Lab 4 references `.env.chaos.example` (lab-04:L320), Lab 7 `tests/budget/report.py` (lab-07:L255): neither exists. `challenges.md:L28–30` specifies `app.guardrails.injection_check → GuardrailResult(flagged, reason, confidence)` and `tests/integration/test_guardrail.py`; the script uses a third name (`northwind.guardrails.looks_like_injection`); the code has `app.mock_llm.looks_like_injection(text) -> bool`.

10. **Production documents were written for a different fixture and still carry a banned API.** `video-generation-plan.md:L132–133`, `recording-guide.md:L122,L133` and `slide-deck-outline.md:L20` use tenants `hr, it, ops, logistics` (code: `ops, finance, hr, eng`, `config.py:L20`), a fixture day `DAY=2026-03-16` with a `--day`/`DAY=` flag and `console/fixtures/day-2026-03-16.sqlite` (none exist; `DEFAULT_DATE = 2026-09-14`, `scenarios.py:L54`), `[NUMBERS]` blocks in every script (zero scripts contain one), `BROKEN=<case>` test toggles (recording-guide:L157) and `LOG_FORMAT=pretty` (recording-guide:L67), none implemented. `slide-deck-outline.md:L68` and `qa-checklist.md:L80` both instruct the designer/QA to show `update_current_trace(session_id=…)`, which curriculum §6 (L348), `langfuse_setup.py:L14` and the README troubleshooting (L104) all say does not exist in langfuse 4.15. The QA checklist would pass a slide the curriculum bans.

Also notable but lower impact: 17 avatar runs exceed the 60-second rule (details in §2); recaps are one sentence everywhere, never the required 3-bullet card; "Welcome back" appears twice (S4:L810, S14:L213); `make report` cannot produce 6.3's showback-by-feature table (it prints a single `chat` row because generation spans carry no `atlas.feature`; the console groups by intent instead).

## (c) Full evidence list

### 1. Flow and curriculum alignment

- **IDs, titles, durations:** every script lecture ID, title and target minute matches the curriculum tables (S1–S15 headers vs curriculum L116–294), including 14.1a. Only cosmetic differences: 15.4 title "Bonus Lecture: keep operating" vs curriculum "Bonus lecture" (required by Udemy, fine); Sections 6–10 use `## Lecture 6.1:` and `### Recap` headings while 1–5 and 11–15 use `## Lecture 6.1 —` and `**Recap:**` (two script templates; `scene_extractor.py` must handle both).
- **Section 1→2 bridge is sound; Section 2→6 numbers are not** (issue 1). S2:L376 ("1,184 conversations", "seed=20260928", "38.2s") vs Makefile defaults and S6:L18 ("10,184 requests in 4,000 sessions"). S5:L331 baseline "empty 6.8 %, grounded 93.2 %" vs S6:L28 grounded 0.94 vs store 0.943.

- **Missing bridges:** nothing introduces the `ga.*` helper module as the agent's actual instrumentation before 6.3; nothing tells the learner that 4.x/5.x code is an alternative to what ships. 9.1's SLO design and 9.5's shipped rules are never reconciled.
- **Curriculum §2 vs repo:** `incidents/incident-04-project/` exists but is absent from curriculum L93–96 (only 01–03 listed); `deploy/prometheus.yml` and `deploy/alerts.yml` exist but are absent from the curriculum tree (L97–102); `tests/unit/` counts "150+" (L104), actual 329 total.
